## 1. THE STRATEGIC CALL (read this first)

Bharat IQ has **zero existing search footprint** — a check of the live SERP for `"Bharat IQ"` returns nothing related to rural insights. That is both the risk and the opportunity:

- **Risk:** nobody searches for "Bharat IQ". Brand-term SEO earns us nothing for 18 months.
- **Opportunity:** the *category* terms are contested by generalists, not specialists. Kantar/NIQ/Hansa/Ipsos own "market research India" but treat rural as a sub-line. Rural activation agencies (Ascent, RW Promotions, India Action Project, Sign Service, Plan Media) own "rural marketing agency" but their insight content is thin blog filler. **Nobody owns "rural intelligence" as a data + knowledge category.**

**Therefore the SEO strategy is not "rank for Bharat IQ". It is: become the default answer to every rural/Bharat market question an Indian marketer, category head or development-sector programme manager types into Google or asks an AI assistant.**

Three simultaneous plays:

| Play | What it does | Timeline to impact |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Category capture** | Rank commercial pages for `rural market research company India`, `rural consumer insights`, `Bharat GTM` | 4–9 months |
| **B. Answer-engine dominance (AEO/GEO)** | Become the cited source inside ChatGPT / Gemini / Perplexity / AI Overviews for rural India data questions | 2–5 months (fastest ROI) |
| **C. Data-asset gravity** | Publish proprietary indices & reports nobody else has → earn links from press, B-school, consulting decks | 6–18 months, compounding |

**The unfair advantage to weaponise:** competitors write *opinions* about rural India. Impact has 27 years of *field data* — real distributor conversations, real retailer counts, real village-level execution records across Bharat. Every page must carry a number a competitor cannot copy.

## 5. TECHNICAL SEO SPEC (hand to the developer)

**Domain:** `bharatiq.in` primary (India ccTLD signals relevance), `bharatiq.com` 301 → `.in`. Alternative: `bharatiq.impactcommunications.in` — **not recommended**, a subdomain splits authority and forfeits an exact-match brand domain.

**Stack:** Next.js (App Router), SSG/ISR — matches the in-house stack in this repo, so the team can maintain it.

**Core Web Vitals targets:** LCP < 2.0s on a 4G Indian mobile connection · INP < 200ms · CLS < 0.1. Test on a ₹10,000 Android over 4G, not a MacBook — a real share of Bharat IQ's own audience (regional distributors, state programme officers) browses that way.

**Must-haves:**
- HTTPS, mobile-first, `hreflang` ready for `/hi/`
- XML sitemap (split: pages / reports / insights) + `robots.txt` + `llms.txt` (AI-crawler manifest: what Bharat IQ is, what it publishes, canonical stat pages)
- Canonicals on every URL; `noindex` on thank-you and gated-asset pages
- Breadcrumbs, visible + `BreadcrumbList` schema
- Images: WebP/AVIF, lazy-loaded, descriptive filenames (`rural-retailer-audit-uttar-pradesh.webp`), keyword-informed alt text — never stuffed
- Self-hosted fonts, preloaded
- No blocking of `GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `Google-Extended` — being quoted by AI assistants is the point

**Schema.org (JSON-LD) per page type:**

| Page | Schema |
|---|---|
| Home | `Organization` + `WebSite` (+ `SearchAction`) |
| Service / Solution | `Service`, `Provider` → Organization |
| Reports | `Report` / `Dataset` ← **`Dataset` is the highest-value, lowest-adoption schema in this category** |
| Insights articles | `Article` + `Author` (real named analysts, with bios — E-E-A-T) |
| FAQ blocks | `FAQPage` |
| Case studies | `Article` + `Review`-free claims only |
| Glossary | `DefinedTerm` + `DefinedTermSet` |
| Contact | `LocalBusiness` with real NAP |

**E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust) — decisive in a YMYL-adjacent data category:**
- Real named authors with photos, credentials, LinkedIn, years in rural research
- "How we know this" methodology note on every data claim
- Sample size, geography, fieldwork dates on every chart
- Physical office address, phone, GST-registered entity, 27-year lineage
- Client logos and named testimonials (with written permission)

## 9. RISKS & HONEST CAVEATS

1. **Name collision.** "Rural Impact Communications" (est. 2018, agri-influencer marketing) is an active, unrelated firm. Searches for "Impact Communications rural" will surface them. Mitigate: consistent entity naming ("Impact Communications, part of Impact Group — est. 1998"), Google Business Profile, Wikidata entry, strong `Organization` schema with `sameAs` links, and a clear About page. **Also check the trademark position before launch.**
2. **"IQ" ambiguity.** `Bharat IQ` currently collides with intelligence-quotient content in search. The brand must always be presented with a qualifier in titles — "Bharat IQ | Rural & Bharat Market Intelligence" — never bare.
3. **Unverified statistics.** All third-party figures in the copy are marked `[VERIFY]` with source and date. **Do not publish a number without re-checking the primary source** — a wrong stat on a data-authority site is fatal, and Impact's own credibility is the product here.
4. **Proof-point blanks.** Copy contains `[FILL: …]` tokens where Impact's real numbers (villages covered, retailers mapped, field executives, client count) belong. These are the single most important conversion elements on the site. **The site should not launch with any token unfilled.** Fabricated scale claims are both an SEO risk and a client-trust risk.
5. **Content debt.** A 160-page site nobody updates ranks worse than a 40-page site kept current. Resource the content engine before expanding the architecture.
6. **This will not work without proprietary data.** If Bharat IQ publishes the same recycled "rural India is 65% of the population" content as everyone else, it ranks nowhere. The moat is Impact's field footprint. Budget for turning it into publishable data.
